# terralayr

> terralayr is an energy-flexibility company that develops, owns and operates utility-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS) and runs LAYR, a cloud platform that aggregates heterogeneous storage assets and makes their flexibility available to utilities, grid operators, power traders, aggregators and asset owners. Storage capacity is bookable via API with no hardware onboarding. The LAYR product API is B2B/private and publishes no public OpenAPI, SDKs or developer documentation.
